- Cell-by-gene expression matrix (sparse, filtered feature-cell matrix)
- Cell segmentation boundaries / polygons
- Transcript-level spatial coordinates (per-transcript detection calls with x/y/z position and assigned gene/cell)
- Morphology image (DAPI/membrane stain, often as OME-TIFF)
- Cell metadata (cell IDs, centroids, area, nucleus/cell boundary info)
- Run/pipeline summary and QC metrics (web summary HTML, metrics CSV)
